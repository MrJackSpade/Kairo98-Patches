# PC-98 translation teams

Human translators and hackers whose English PC-98 work we know of. Their translations always take precedence over this repo's machine translations: we link to them, skip games they've announced, and retire our version of any game they release.

Built from the credits in the [archive.org PC-98 translation archive](https://archive.org/details/nec-pc-9801-translations) plus team pages (`tools/build_teams.py`). Please support the teams directly.
